# CattleCare Excluded Research Data Archive

This directory maintains the catalog and research provenance of dataset modalities that were excluded from the CattleCare hardware-compatible training dataset due to physical hardware constraints (DHT11, MPU6050, ESP32 neckband collar).

All original raw files are preserved intact in sensor_data/.

Refer to research_archive_manifest.csv and data/analysis/exclusion_log.csv for detailed technical justifications.
